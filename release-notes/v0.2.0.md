Lastmark is now set up by each person for their own day. Nothing is pre-filled.

## New
- **First-run setup.** Name, focus and break length, priorities per day, reminders, calendar and drift shield strictness, plus your first priorities.
- **Tasks.** Add, edit, delete and complete tasks. Star tasks to make them today's priorities. Each task can hold a next step.
- **Plan today.** Pick your priorities for the day, in order. Unfinished priorities carry over to tomorrow.
- **Focus that works.** Resume focus starts a timer. Pause, take a break, mark done or stop.
- **Save your place.** Before each break, write your next step. It's the first thing you see when you come back.
- **Done check.** When a block ends: finished, keep going, or save your place and take a break.
- **Reminders.** Plan my day at a time you choose, break over, and repeat nudges if you haven't come back.
- **Calendar.** Shows today's events from any calendar synced to your phone (Google, Outlook, Samsung). No sign-in needed.
- **Drift shield.** Choose your distracting apps and a strictness level. During a focus block, opening one shows your task and next step on top.
- **Settings** for all of the above, plus reset and run setup again.

## Install
Download `lastmark.apk` below on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or later) and open it. If you had v0.1.0, it installs over the top.
